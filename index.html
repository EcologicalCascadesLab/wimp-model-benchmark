WildObs WIMP model benchmark

Automated benchmarking of the computer-vision models on the WildObs Image Management Platform (MegaDetector thresholds and species classifiers) against human-validated camera-trap images, extending Colin Broughton's model-benchmark-testing-workflow.

Test sets

  • By sequence: Australian Wet Tropics, 3 November 2026. 4,399 images (up to 2 per validated sequence), 19 classes, 5 model runs (findings and caveats)
  • By image: the same 2,905 sequences, one image each (mostly the middle image), 19 classes, 5 model runs (findings and comparison with by sequence)
